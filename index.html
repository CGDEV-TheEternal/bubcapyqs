About

Burdwan University BCA PYQS is an unofficial platform created to make previous-year question papers easier to find and access for BCA students of Burdwan University.

The question papers are collected and shared for educational and reference purposes. To download a previous year question paper, use the Download section. If you have a question paper that's missing from the collection, you can contribute it through the Upload section and help juniors.

This platform is not affiliated with Burdwan University.
